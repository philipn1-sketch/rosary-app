Praying

Pray with the guided Rosary takes you through every prayer and bead, with the words written out. Tap anywhere for the next bead. If you close it, it remembers your place. Mark today as prayed is for when you pray with your own beads.

Today's mysteries follow the weekly schedule you chose in setup; tap Change to pray a different set. Every prayer is written out under the book icon.

Streak and calendar

Your streak counts the days in a row you've prayed. It stays active until the end of the day after you last prayed. Tap any past day on the calendar to log it or edit its intention. You can hide the streak in Settings.

Prayer groups

Tap the people icon and sign in with Apple or Google. Create a group and tap Invite people to send its 6-character code, or Join with a code someone sent you. Members see who has prayed today and can share prayer requests; tap I prayed to let someone know you prayed for them. Press and hold a request to report it or block its author.

Widgets, reminders and backup

Add the Rosary widget from your Home Screen or Lock Screen; tap the number to mark today as prayed. Turn on an evening reminder in Settings. Export your history as a CSV file in Settings to keep a backup, and import it on a new phone.

Deleting your account

Tap the people icon, then Delete my account. This removes everything stored for prayer groups. Your prayer history on your phone isn't affected.
